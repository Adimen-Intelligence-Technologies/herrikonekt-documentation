title: "Complete the Adimen AI Onboarding Flow Step by Step"
sidebarTitle: "Onboarding"
description: "Walk through the Adimen AI onboarding flow: setting preferences, granting permissions, connecting services, and personalizing your experience."
---

The first time you open Adimen AI after creating your account, a short onboarding flow helps you configure the app to fit your needs. Each step takes only a moment, and the choices you make here shape how the AI personalizes your experience from the start. Nothing is locked in — you can adjust every setting later.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Language & Region">
    Select your preferred language and region from the list. This setting controls the language used in AI responses, date and number formats, and the region used to surface locally relevant content and news. You can change both later in **Settings > Language & Region**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Notification Permissions">
    Grant notification permissions so Adimen AI can send you proactive suggestions, automation alerts, and reminders. Tap **Allow** when prompted by your device.

    If you prefer to review notification types first, tap **Not Now** — you can enable notifications later in **Settings > Notifications**. See the [Notifications guide](/guides/notifications) for a full breakdown of notification types and frequency controls.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Microphone Access (Optional)">
    Grant microphone access to use voice input in the AI chat. Tap **Allow** if you want to speak your requests instead of typing them.

    This step is fully optional. If you prefer text-only input or want to decide later, tap **Skip** — the app works completely without microphone access. You can grant permission at any time through your device's Settings app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Your Services">
    Connect external services to give Adimen AI access to relevant context, such as your calendar events, email summaries, or third-party app data. Available integrations include:

    - Google Calendar
    - Gmail
    - Microsoft Outlook
    - And more via Settings > Integrations

    Tap **Connect** next to any service you want to link, or tap **Skip** to continue without connecting anything. You can add or remove integrations at any time from **Settings > Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Focus Areas">
    Select the topics that matter most to you — for example, productivity, health & fitness, technology, news, or finance. Adimen AI uses these preferences to personalize the recommendations and contextual cards shown on your home screen.

    Choose as many or as few as you like. You can update your focus areas later under **Settings > Personalization**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Exploring">
    Onboarding is complete. Adimen AI takes you to the home screen, where your personalized recommendations are already loading. Tap the chat bubble to open your first AI session and start asking questions right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All permissions granted during onboarding — including notifications, microphone, and integrations — can be changed at any time through your device's Settings app or inside Adimen AI under Settings.
</Note>

<Info>
  If you want to go through the onboarding flow again — for example, after a reset or to reconfigure your preferences — you can re-run it by navigating to **Settings > Account > Redo Onboarding**.
</Info>
