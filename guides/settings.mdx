The Settings screen is your control center for everything in Adimen AI. From updating your profile and managing your subscription to tweaking the app's appearance and reviewing active sessions, every configuration option lives here. Open Settings by tapping your profile avatar in the top-right corner of the home screen.

<Accordion title="Account">
  Manage your personal profile and account-level options.

  | Setting | Description |
  |---|---|
  | **Profile Photo** | Upload or change your avatar image |
  | **Display Name** | Update the name shown on your profile and in shared activity |
  | **Email** | View or update the email address associated with your account |
  | **Subscription** | View your current plan, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel |
  | **Export Data** | Download a copy of your data, including conversation history and saved items |
  | **Delete Account** | Permanently delete your account and all associated data |

  To export your data, tap **Export Data** and choose a format. You will receive a download link via email within a few minutes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Privacy">
  Control what data Adimen AI stores and how it is used to personalize your experience.

  | Setting | Description |
  |---|---|
  | **Clear Conversation History** | Immediately and permanently delete all stored AI conversations |
  | **Personalization** | Toggle whether Adimen AI uses your activity to personalize recommendations |
  | **Data Sharing** | Manage whether anonymized usage data is shared to improve the product |
  | **Privacy Policy** | Opens the full Adimen AI Privacy Policy at [adimen.ai/privacy](https://adimen.ai/privacy) |

  For a full explanation of data collection and retention, see the [Data Privacy](/concepts/data-privacy) guide.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Notifications">
  Configure which alerts you receive and when they are delivered.

  | Setting | Description |
  |---|---|
  | **Notification Types** | Toggle each category on or off: Proactive Suggestions, Automation Alerts, Sync Alerts, Security Alerts, Weekly Digest |
  | **Quiet Hours** | Set a time window during which non-critical notifications are silenced |
  | **Suggestion Frequency** | Control how many Proactive Suggestion notifications you receive per day |

  For a complete walkthrough of notification options, see the [Notifications guide](/guides/notifications).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Integrations">
  View and manage the external services connected to your Adimen AI account.

  - The **Connected Services** list shows every integration that is currently active, along with its last-sync time and status.
  - Tap **Add Integration** to connect a new service such as Google Calendar, Gmail, or Microsoft Outlook.
  - Tap any active integration and select **Disconnect** to remove it. Disconnecting a service removes its data access immediately but does not delete any data already synced.

  For a full list of supported integrations and connection instructions, see the [Integrations Overview](/integrations/overview).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Security">
  Protect your account with two-factor authentication and manage active login sessions.

  | Setting | Description |
  |---|---|
  | **Two-Factor Authentication** | Enable TOTP (authenticator app) or SMS-based 2FA for your account |
  | **Active Sessions** | View a list of all devices where your account is currently logged in, including device type and last-seen time |
  | **Revoke Access** | Sign out a specific session remotely (for example, a lost or old device) or sign out all sessions at once |

  To enable 2FA, tap **Two-Factor Authentication** and follow the guided setup. See [Account Setup](/guides/account-setup#two-factor-authentication) for step-by-step instructions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Appearance">
  Customize how the app looks and feels.

  | Setting | Options |
  |---|---|
  | **Theme** | Light / Dark / System (follows your device setting) |
  | **Font Size** | Small / Medium (default) / Large |
  | **Accent Color** | Choose from a set of preset accent colors to personalize the UI |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Language & Region">
  Set the language and regional formats used throughout the app.

  | Setting | Description |
  |---|---|
  | **App Language** | Choose the language for the app interface and AI responses |
  | **Date & Time Format** | Select your preferred date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY or DD/MM/YYYY) and 12-hour or 24-hour clock |
  | **Region** | Set your region to influence locally relevant content, news, and suggestions |
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="About">
  Find app information and provide feedback.

  | Item | Description |
  |---|---|
  | **App Version** | Displays the current installed version of Adimen AI |
  | **Terms of Service** | Opens the Adimen AI Terms of Service |
  | **Privacy Policy** | Opens the Adimen AI Privacy Policy |
  | **Rate the App** | Opens the App Store or Google Play listing to leave a review |
  | **Send Feedback** | Opens an in-app feedback form to report issues or suggest improvements |
</Accordion>

<Note>
  Some settings — including profile updates, integration changes, and subscription modifications — require an active internet connection to save. Changes made offline will not be applied until connectivity is restored.
</Note>
