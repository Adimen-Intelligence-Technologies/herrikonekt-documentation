Adimen AI packs a full suite of intelligent features into a single mobile app — from natural conversation and proactive recommendations to powerful automations and a unified search experience. This guide covers every major feature so you know exactly what is available and how to use it.

## AI Chat

AI Chat is the heart of the Adimen AI experience. To start a new session, tap the **chat bubble icon** on the home screen. A fresh session opens immediately.

- **Type or dictate** your request in the input field. Tap the microphone icon to switch to voice input (requires microphone permission).
- The AI responds with **text, links, or actionable cards** — for example, a card might let you accept a calendar suggestion or trigger an action directly from the response.
- **Long-press any response** to reveal options: copy the text, share it, save it as a bookmark, or report a problem.
- Follow-up questions are understood in context, so you can refine or build on your previous message without repeating yourself.

Sessions expire after 24 hours of inactivity. To learn more about how context and memory work, see [AI Features](/concepts/ai-features).

## Smart Recommendations

The home screen shows a dynamic feed of contextual cards surfaced by the AI based on your schedule, recent activity, connected integrations, and chosen focus areas.

- **Swipe left** on a card to dismiss it and signal that it is not relevant to you.
- **Tap "Feedback"** on any card to send a thumbs-up or thumbs-down rating — this directly improves future recommendations.
- Cards are refreshed throughout the day as new signals come in, such as an upcoming calendar event or a trending topic in your focus areas.

You can adjust the topics that drive recommendations under **Settings > Personalization**.

## Automations

Access **Automations** from the bottom navigation bar (the lightning bolt icon). Automations let you define trigger-action rules that run automatically in the background.

Use the **visual rule builder** to compose rules without writing any code:

1. Tap **New Automation**.
2. Choose a **trigger** — a time, an event, or an external condition (for example, "Every Monday at 9 AM" or "When a new email arrives from a specific sender").
3. Add one or more **actions** — for example, "Summarize my emails" or "Send a push notification."
4. Tap **Save & Enable**.

Example automations to get you started:

- *Every Monday morning, summarize my unread emails and show them on my home screen.*
- *When I open the app, show today's top news in my focus areas.*
- *When a new calendar event is added, set a reminder 30 minutes before.*

Tap any existing automation to edit it, disable it temporarily, or delete it.

## Search

Tap the **search icon** (magnifying glass) in the top navigation bar to open unified search. A single query searches across:

- Your **conversation history**
- **Saved items** (bookmarked responses)
- Data from your **connected integrations** (for example, calendar events or emails)

Results are grouped by source and ranked by relevance. Tap any result to open it in context.

## Saved Items

To save an AI response for later, **long-press** the message and tap **Save**. Saved items are stored in your personal bookmark library.

Access your saved items at any time by tapping the **bookmark icon** in the top navigation bar. From the saved items view, you can:

- Search and filter by date or keyword
- Open the original conversation context
- Delete individual saved items or clear all at once

Saved items persist across sessions and are synced to your account, so they are available even if you switch devices.

## Activity Dashboard

Get a clear picture of how you use Adimen AI by opening the **Activity Dashboard**. Navigate there via **Profile > Activity**.

The dashboard shows:

- **Usage stats** — Total requests this week, daily average, and streak
- **Request history** — A searchable log of past AI sessions and queries
- **Integration activity** — Status and recent events for each connected service, including successful syncs and any errors
