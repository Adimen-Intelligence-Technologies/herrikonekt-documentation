Before you can use Adimen AI, you need an account. This guide walks you through creating your account, choosing the right subscription plan, managing billing, and securing your profile with two-factor authentication. The whole process takes just a few minutes.

## Creating Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the App">
    Launch the Adimen AI app on your iOS or Android device. If this is your first time opening the app, you will be taken directly to the welcome screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Sign Up">
    On the welcome screen, tap **Sign Up** to create a new account. If you already have an account, tap **Log In** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Email or Social Login">
    Select how you want to register:
    - **Email** — Enter your email address and create a password.
    - **Continue with Google** — Authenticate with your Google account.
    - **Continue with Apple** — Authenticate with your Apple ID (iOS only).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email">
    If you signed up with email, check your inbox for a verification email from Adimen AI and tap the confirmation link. The link expires after 24 hours. If you do not see the email, check your spam folder or tap **Resend Email** in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Display Name and Avatar">
    Enter a display name and optionally upload a profile photo. These appear in your profile and in any shared activity. You can update both at any time under Settings > Account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Subscription Plans

Adimen AI offers three plans to match different levels of use. All plans include access to the core app features; advanced capabilities unlock on Pro and Business.

| Feature | Free | Pro | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI capability | Basic | Advanced | Advanced |
| Daily requests | 50 requests/day | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Devices | 1 device | 5 devices | Unlimited |
| Priority support | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Team features | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| API access | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| SLA guarantee | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |

<Tip>
  If you use Adimen AI daily for work or productivity tasks, **Pro** gives you the best value — unlimited requests, multi-device support, and access to advanced AI capabilities at a flat monthly rate.
</Tip>

## Managing Your Subscription

You can view and change your subscription plan at any time by navigating to **Settings > Subscription** inside the app. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel directly from that screen.

Billing is handled entirely through the platform store:
- **iOS** — Managed via your Apple ID and the App Store.
- **Android** — Managed via your Google account and Google Play.

Because billing runs through the stores, Adimen AI does not store your payment information. For refund requests or billing disputes, contact Apple Support or Google Play Support directly.

## Two-Factor Authentication

Adding two-factor authentication (2FA) to your account significantly reduces the risk of unauthorized access. To enable it:

1. Go to **Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication**.
2. Tap **Enable 2FA**.
3. Choose your preferred method:
   - **Authenticator App (TOTP)** — Scan the QR code with an app like Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password. Enter the 6-digit code to confirm.
   - **SMS** — Enter your phone number and verify the code sent via text message.
4. Save your backup codes in a secure location. These codes let you regain access if you lose your authentication device.

Once 2FA is enabled, you will be prompted for a verification code each time you log in on a new device.
