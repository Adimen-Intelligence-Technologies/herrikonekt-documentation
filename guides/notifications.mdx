Adimen AI sends push notifications to keep you informed and productive — from proactive AI suggestions to automation results and security alerts. You have full control over which notifications you receive, when they are delivered, and how frequently they appear. This guide walks you through every notification setting.

## Notification Types

Adimen AI supports five categories of notifications. Each can be toggled independently so you only receive what is relevant to you.

| Notification Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Proactive Suggestions** | The AI surfaces timely reminders, recommendations, and contextual nudges based on your schedule and focus areas |
| **Automation Alerts** | Sent when an automation rule triggers and completes an action (or encounters an error) |
| **Sync Alerts** | Notifies you when a connected integration successfully syncs or when a sync fails and requires attention |
| **Security Alerts** | Sent for new login activity, 2FA verification codes, and other account security events |
| **Weekly Digest** | A weekly summary of your Adimen AI usage, including request stats, saved items, and automation activity |

## Enabling Notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notification Settings">
    In the Adimen AI app, go to **Settings > Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Notification Types">
    Use the individual toggles to enable or disable each notification category. Changes take effect immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Device Permission">
    If this is your first time enabling notifications and you previously denied permission, a prompt will appear asking you to update your device-level setting. Tap **Open Settings** and enable notifications for Adimen AI in your device's Settings app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quiet Hours

Set a **Quiet Hours** window to pause non-critical notifications during times when you do not want to be interrupted — for example, overnight or during focused work blocks.

To configure Quiet Hours:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Notifications > Quiet Hours**.
2. Toggle **Enable Quiet Hours** on.
3. Set your **Start Time** and **End Time**.

During a quiet hours window, Adimen AI suppresses Proactive Suggestions, Automation Alerts, Sync Alerts, and Weekly Digest notifications.

**Security Alerts are always delivered**, regardless of quiet hours. These include login alerts and 2FA codes, which should never be delayed.

<Tip>
  Set Quiet Hours to match your sleep schedule or your deepest focus periods. Even a simple overnight window (10 PM – 7 AM) can meaningfully reduce interruptions and help you stay in flow during the day.
</Tip>

## Notification Frequency

For **Proactive Suggestions** specifically, you can control how many notifications you receive per day. Choose a frequency level that matches your preference:

| Frequency Level | Notifications Per Day |
|---|---|
| **Off** | None |
| **Low** | 1–2 per day |
| **Normal** | Up to 5 per day |
| **High** | Unlimited |

To set the frequency, go to **Settings > Notifications > Proactive Suggestions > Frequency** and select your preferred level.

<Note>
  Notification delivery ultimately depends on your device-level permissions. If notifications are not arriving, check that Adimen AI has notification permission enabled in your device's Settings app under **Apps > Adimen AI > Notifications**.
</Note>
