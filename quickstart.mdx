---
title: "Adimen AI Quickstart: Get Up and Running in Minutes"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Download the Adimen AI app, create your account, complete onboarding, and make your first AI-assisted request in just a few steps."
---

This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a fresh install to your first AI-powered interaction. By the end, you'll have the Adimen AI app installed on your device, your account configured, and hands-on experience with the core features — all in under ten minutes.

<Note>
  If you already have an Adimen AI account, open the app and tap **Log In** to skip straight to the features you need.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the App">
    Adimen AI is available on both iOS and Android. Choose your platform and install the app from the official store.

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Download for iOS" icon="apple" href="https://apps.apple.com">
        Available on the **App Store** for iPhone and iPad running iOS 15 or later.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Download for Android" icon="android" href="https://play.google.com">
        Available on **Google Play** for devices running Android 8.0 or later.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Open the app after installation and tap **Create Account** on the welcome screen. You can sign up with:

    - **Email address** — enter your email, choose a password, and verify your inbox
    - **Google** — tap **Continue with Google** and authorize the app
    - **Apple** — tap **Continue with Apple** and authenticate with Face ID or your Apple ID

    <Note>
      Email sign-ups require inbox verification before you can access AI features. Check your spam folder if the verification email doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete Onboarding">
    After logging in for the first time, Adimen AI guides you through a short onboarding flow to personalize your experience. You'll configure:

    - **Language** — choose the primary language for AI responses
    - **Notification preferences** — select which alerts and summaries you want to receive
    - **Connected services** — optionally link third-party apps such as your calendar, email, or task manager

    You can update all of these settings later under **Settings > Preferences** at any time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make Your First AI Request">
    Once onboarding is complete, you land on the Adimen AI home screen.

    1. Tap the **chat icon** in the bottom navigation bar.
    2. Type a question or command in the input field — for example:
       ```text
       Summarize my emails from today
       ```
    3. Adimen AI processes your request and displays a response in real time.

    Try a few different commands to explore what the AI can do. You can ask questions, request summaries, set reminders, and more.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Sync">
    Turn on cloud sync so your conversation history, preferences, and integrations stay consistent across all your devices.

    1. Open **Settings** from the bottom navigation bar.
    2. Tap **Account**.
    3. Toggle **Cloud Sync** to **On**.

    Once enabled, any device signed in to the same Adimen AI account automatically stays in sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Enable push notifications when prompted — Adimen AI uses notifications to deliver proactive summaries, reminders, and real-time alerts, giving you the best possible experience.
</Tip>
