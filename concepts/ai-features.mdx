Adimen AI is built around a powerful AI engine that understands what you need, anticipates what comes next, and takes action on your behalf. Whether you are typing a quick question, setting up a recurring automation, or asking a follow-up mid-conversation, every interaction is designed to feel natural and productive.

## Natural Language Understanding

You do not need to learn special commands or keywords to get results. Type or speak your requests in plain, everyday language and Adimen AI figures out what you mean. The assistant understands intent, resolves ambiguity, and handles follow-up questions within the same session — so you can refine your request naturally without starting over.

For example, you can ask:
- "What's on my calendar tomorrow?"
- "Actually, make that next Friday."
- "And send a reminder to my team the day before."

Each follow-up is understood in context of what came before, just like a conversation with a person.

## Smart Recommendations

Adimen AI learns from your usage patterns and connected integrations to surface relevant suggestions before you even ask. These recommendations appear as contextual cards on your home screen and are updated throughout the day based on your schedule, past requests, and real-time signals from connected services.

Recommendations improve over time as the assistant builds a clearer picture of your habits and preferences. You can refine them by dismissing irrelevant cards or selecting your focus areas in the onboarding flow or under Settings > Personalization.

## Automated Tasks

The **Automations** tab lets you create "if this, then that" rules that run in the background without any manual input. Use the visual rule builder to define a trigger and one or more actions.

Common automation examples include:

- **Summarize daily news** — Every morning at 8 AM, fetch and summarize top headlines in your chosen categories.
- **Send reminders** — When a calendar event is added, send a reminder notification 30 minutes before.
- **Sync calendar events** — When a new task is created in a connected app, automatically add it to your calendar.

You can build, edit, and pause automations at any time. See the [Main Features guide](/guides/main-features) for a full walkthrough of the Automations interface.

## Contextual Memory

Within a single session, Adimen AI remembers everything you have discussed — earlier questions, context you have provided, and actions you have taken. This means you can refer back to previous parts of a conversation without repeating yourself.

Sessions expire after **24 hours of inactivity**. Once a session expires, the assistant starts fresh with no memory of the prior exchange. Conversation history is stored for 30 days by default; see the [Data Privacy](/concepts/data-privacy) page for full details on data retention and your privacy controls.

<Note>
  AI feature availability may vary depending on your subscription plan. Some advanced capabilities — including extended automation rules and priority AI responses — are available on Pro and Business plans only.
</Note>
