Your privacy is a core part of how Adimen AI is designed — not an afterthought. This page explains exactly what data is collected, how it is protected, how long it is kept, and the controls you have to manage it. If you have questions beyond what is covered here, refer to the full privacy policy linked at the bottom of this page.

## Data Encryption

Every piece of data that travels between your device and Adimen AI servers is protected using **TLS 1.3**, the current industry standard for transport security. Data stored on Adimen AI servers is encrypted at rest using **AES-256** encryption. This means your conversations and personal information are protected both in motion and when stored.

## What Data Is Collected

Adimen AI collects only the data necessary to provide and improve the service. Here is a breakdown of what is collected and why:

| Data Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Usage analytics** (anonymized) | Understand how features are used to improve the product |
| **AI request content** | Improve AI response quality and relevance |
| **Device metadata** (OS version, app version) | Ensure compatibility and diagnose issues |

**Adimen AI does not collect advertising data and never sells your data to third parties.**

## Data Retention

AI conversation history is retained for **30 days by default**. After that period, conversations are automatically and permanently deleted from Adimen AI servers.

You can delete your conversation history at any time before the 30-day period by navigating to **Settings > Privacy > Clear History**.

Account data — such as your profile, subscription information, and preferences — is retained for as long as your account remains active. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed.

## Your Privacy Controls

You have direct control over your personal data through the app. The following controls are available:

| Control | Location in App |
|---|---|
| Delete conversation history | Settings > Privacy > Clear History |
| Disable personalization | Settings > Privacy > Personalization |
| Download your data | Settings > Account > Export Data |
| Delete your account | Settings > Account > Delete Account |

<Warning>
  Deleting your account permanently removes all of your data — including conversation history, saved items, automations, and account information. This action cannot be undone and your data cannot be recovered after deletion.
</Warning>
