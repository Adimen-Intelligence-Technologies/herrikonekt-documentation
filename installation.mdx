Installing Adimen AI takes just a couple of minutes on both iOS and Android. This guide covers system requirements, platform-specific installation steps, how to keep the app up to date, and solutions to the most common installation problems.

<Warning>
  Only install Adimen AI from the official Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Sideloading the app from unofficial sources exposes your device to security risks and is not supported — your account may be suspended if unofficial builds are detected.
</Warning>

## System Requirements

Make sure your device meets the minimum requirements before installing.

| Requirement | iOS | Android |
|---|---|---|
| **Operating System** | iOS 15 or later | Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later |
| **Device** | iPhone 8 or later | Any device with 2 GB RAM minimum |
| **Free Storage** | 150 MB | 150 MB |
| **Internet Connection** | Required for AI features | Required for AI features |

## Installation Steps

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the App Store">
        On your iPhone or iPad, tap the **App Store** icon on your home screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Search for Adimen AI">
        Tap the **Search** tab at the bottom of the screen, then type **Adimen AI** in the search bar and tap **Search** on your keyboard.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Get">
        Locate the Adimen AI listing in the results and tap **Get**. The button changes to an install progress indicator.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authenticate">
        Confirm the installation with **Face ID**, **Touch ID**, or your **Apple ID password** when prompted.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the App">
        Once the download finishes, tap **Open** from the App Store or tap the Adimen AI icon that appears on your home screen. Follow the on-screen setup to create or log in to your account.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Google Play">
        On your Android device, tap the **Google Play** icon in your app drawer or on your home screen.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Search for Adimen AI">
        Tap the **search bar** at the top of the screen, type **Adimen AI**, and tap the search icon or press **Enter**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tap Install">
        Tap the Adimen AI listing in the results, then tap **Install**. The app downloads and installs automatically.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the App">
        When installation is complete, tap **Open** from the Google Play page or tap the Adimen AI icon in your app drawer. Follow the on-screen setup to create or log in to your account.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Updating the App

Keeping Adimen AI up to date ensures you have the latest AI improvements, new features, and security patches.

**On iOS:** Open the **App Store**, tap your profile icon in the top-right corner, scroll to **Available Updates**, and tap **Update** next to Adimen AI. You can also enable automatic updates under **Settings > App Store > App Updates**.

**On Android:** Open **Google Play**, tap your profile icon, go to **Manage apps & device > Updates available**, and tap **Update** next to Adimen AI. To enable automatic updates, open the Adimen AI listing, tap the three-dot menu, and select **Enable auto update**.

## Troubleshooting Installation Issues

<Accordion title="Troubleshooting Installation Issues">
  **App not found in the store**

  Adimen AI may not be available in all regions yet. Check that your App Store or Google Play account is set to a supported region. On iOS, go to **Settings > [Your Name] > Media & Purchases > Country/Region**. On Android, open Google Play **Settings > Account and device preferences > Country and profiles**.

  ---

  **Installation fails or stalls**

  A failed installation is usually caused by insufficient storage. Confirm your device has at least **150 MB of free space** and try again. If the problem persists, restart your device, then retry the installation.

  ---

  **App crashes immediately after launch**

  An immediate crash typically means your operating system is below the minimum required version. Update your OS to at least **iOS 15** or **Android 8.0** and then relaunch Adimen AI. If you're already on a supported OS version, uninstall the app, restart your device, and reinstall from the store.
</Accordion>
