Adimen AI automatically keeps your experience consistent across every device you use. When you add an automation on your phone, it appears on your tablet. When you save an item on your desktop, it's ready on your phone. This page explains what gets synced, how to manage your sync settings, and what to expect when you're offline or switching devices.

<Note>
  Cloud sync requires an active Adimen AI account. If you use the app in guest mode, all data is stored locally on your device only and will not be available on other devices or after uninstalling the app.
</Note>

## What Gets Synced

The following data syncs automatically across all your signed-in devices:

- **Conversation history** — all AI chat sessions and their content
- **Saved items** — bookmarked responses, saved searches, and pinned content
- **Automation rules** — any automations you have created or enabled
- **Integration connections** — your linked third-party services and their permission scopes
- **Notification preferences** — which events trigger notifications and how they are delivered
- **Appearance settings** — theme, font size, and layout preferences

**Not synced (device-specific):**

- Local notification schedules tied to device-level system settings
- Biometric authentication settings (Face ID, fingerprint)
- Offline-cached content specific to a device's storage

## Enabling Sync

Sync is on by default as soon as you sign in to your Adimen AI account. To verify that sync is active on your current device:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Go to **Settings > Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Cloud Sync">
    Locate the **Cloud Sync** toggle. If it is off, tap it to enable sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Initial Sync">
    On a new device, the first sync may take up to a few minutes depending on the amount of data in your account. A spinning sync icon in the top bar indicates sync is in progress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Multi-Device Limits

The number of devices you can have signed in simultaneously depends on your plan:

| Plan | Active Devices |
|---|---|
| Free | 1 device |
| Pro | Up to 5 devices |
| Business | Unlimited devices |

To view all devices currently signed in to your account, go to **Settings > Security > Active Sessions**. From there you can:

- See each device's name, model, and last active time
- Sign out of individual devices remotely
- Sign out of all other devices at once

<Tip>
  If you reach your device limit, sign out of an unused device in **Settings > Security > Active Sessions** before signing in on a new one — this avoids being prompted to sign out unexpectedly.
</Tip>

## Sync Conflicts

Sync conflicts occur when the same setting or piece of data is changed on two devices before either change has synced. Adimen AI resolves conflicts as follows:

- **Settings and preferences** — the most recent change wins, based on the timestamp recorded at the moment you made the change.
- **Conversation history** — changes are merged, not overwritten. Messages from both devices are combined into a single chronological history.
- **Automation rules** — the most recently modified version of a rule is kept. If a rule was deleted on one device and edited on another, the deletion takes precedence.

In practice, conflicts are rare because sync happens in near real time over an active connection.

## Offline Behavior

Adimen AI is designed to remain useful even without an internet connection:

- The app loads your last-synced state, so your conversation history, saved items, and settings are all accessible.
- Changes you make while offline — such as updating a preference or saving an item — are queued locally.
- When your connection is restored, the app automatically uploads your queued changes and downloads any updates from other devices.
- A **sync pending** icon appears in the top navigation bar whenever there are unsynced local changes. Tapping it shows a summary of what is waiting to sync.

<Info>
  AI chat features require an active internet connection and will not work offline. Locally cached content and navigation remain available.
</Info>
