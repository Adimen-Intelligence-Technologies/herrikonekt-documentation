Integrations let Adimen AI reach across the apps you already use every day — your calendar, email, project tools, and communication platforms — so it can surface relevant context, automate repetitive tasks, and give you smarter recommendations without switching between apps. Once connected, Adimen AI pulls only the data it needs and surfaces it exactly when it's useful to you.

## How Integrations Work

When you connect a service, Adimen AI follows the principle of least privilege: it requests only the permissions required for the features you enable. For example, connecting Google Calendar grants read-only access to your events by default — Adimen AI never reads data outside the scope you approve.

You stay in full control at all times. You can revoke any integration at any time from **Settings > Integrations**, and Adimen AI will immediately stop accessing that service's data.

## Adding an Integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations Settings">
    In the Adimen AI app, navigate to **Settings > Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add Integration">
    Tap the **Add Integration** button at the top of the Integrations screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Service">
    Choose the service you want to connect from the list of available integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign In and Grant Permissions">
    You will be redirected to the service's own sign-in and authorization screen. Review the requested permissions and tap **Allow** (or the equivalent) to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Connection">
    After authorizing, you are returned to Adimen AI and the new integration appears in your integrations list with a green connected indicator.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Permissions

Each integration card in **Settings > Integrations** shows a summary of the permissions currently granted. Tap any integration to open its detail view, where you can:

- Review the exact data scopes Adimen AI can access
- Expand or restrict permissions (where supported by the service)
- Disconnect the integration entirely

Updating a permission scope may redirect you back to the third-party service's authorization screen to re-confirm the change.

<Tip>
  Start by connecting your calendar and email. These two integrations unlock the most impactful smart recommendations — such as meeting preparation briefs, schedule conflict alerts, and email-to-task creation — and give Adimen AI the context it needs to be genuinely helpful from day one.
</Tip>
