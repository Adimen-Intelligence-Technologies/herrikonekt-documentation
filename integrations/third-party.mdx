Adimen AI connects to the services at the center of your workflow through secure, permission-scoped integrations. Each integration uses industry-standard OAuth 2.0 or webhook-based authorization, so your credentials are never stored by Adimen AI. The sections below walk you through setting up each supported service and explain exactly what data Adimen AI can access once connected.

<Warning>
  When you disconnect an integration in Adimen AI, also revoke access from the source service's own permissions page (for example, **Google Account > Security > Third-party apps**, or your Microsoft account's **App permissions** page). Removing the integration in Adimen AI alone stops the app from using the connection, but the authorization grant may remain active on the service side until you revoke it there as well.
</Warning>

## Service Setup Guides

<Accordion title="Google Workspace (Calendar, Gmail, Drive)">
  Google Workspace integration gives Adimen AI access to your calendar events, email metadata, and Drive file names through a standard OAuth 2.0 flow.

  **To connect your Google account:**

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Integrations">
      Go to **Settings > Integrations > Add Integration** and select **Google**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Sign In">
      Sign in with the Google account you want to connect. If you manage multiple Google accounts, make sure you select the correct one.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Select Permission Scopes">
      Choose which Google services to enable — Calendar, Gmail, Drive, or a combination. Review the permissions listed on the Google consent screen, then tap **Allow**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm">
      You are returned to Adimen AI. Your Google integration now appears as connected.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  **Available data:**
  - **Google Calendar** — event titles, times, attendees, and locations
  - **Gmail** — email metadata (subject, sender, date); email body content is never read
  - **Google Drive** — file and folder names; file content is not accessed

  <Note>
    All Google scopes are read-only by default. If you want Adimen AI to create calendar events on your behalf (for example, via automations), you must grant write access to Google Calendar when prompted during setup, or update the scope later in **Settings > Integrations > Google > Edit Permissions**.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Teams, OneDrive)">
  Microsoft 365 integration connects Adimen AI to your Outlook calendar and email, Teams notifications, and OneDrive file list using Microsoft's OAuth 2.0 flow.

  **To connect your Microsoft account:**

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Integrations">
      Go to **Settings > Integrations > Add Integration** and select **Microsoft 365**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Sign In">
      Sign in with your Microsoft personal or work/school account. For work accounts managed by an organization, your IT admin may need to pre-approve the Adimen AI application.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Grant Permissions">
      Review the requested permissions on the Microsoft consent screen and tap **Accept**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm">
      Return to Adimen AI — your Microsoft 365 integration is now listed as connected.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  **Available data:**
  - **Outlook** — calendar events (title, time, attendees) and email metadata (subject, sender, date)
  - **Microsoft Teams** — notifications and mentions in channels and chats
  - **OneDrive** — file and folder names (content is not read)

  <Note>
    If you use a work or school Microsoft account, your organization's admin must grant tenant-wide consent before you can complete the connection. Contact your IT department if the authorization step fails with an admin approval error.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Slack">
  The Slack integration lets Adimen AI send you notifications directly in Slack and, in channels where the Adimen AI bot has been added, read messages to provide context-aware summaries.

  **To connect Slack:**

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Integrations">
      Go to **Settings > Integrations > Add Integration** and select **Slack**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Choose Your Workspace">
      Sign in to the Slack workspace you want to connect. You must have permission to add apps to your workspace.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Authorize the Bot">
      Review the bot permissions on the Slack authorization screen and click **Allow**. Adimen AI will request permission to send messages and read messages in channels it is invited to.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Add the Bot to Channels">
      In Slack, invite the Adimen AI bot to any channel where you want it to operate: type `/invite @AdimenAI` in the channel.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  **Available data:**
  - Messages in channels where the Adimen AI bot has been explicitly added
  - Direct message notifications (the bot can send DMs to you)

  <Note>
    Slack workspace admin approval is required before other members of your workspace can add the Adimen AI bot. If you are not an admin, request approval through your Slack admin or IT team.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Notion">
  The Notion integration uses Notion's OAuth flow to connect specific pages and databases from your workspace. You choose exactly which content Adimen AI can access.

  **To connect Notion:**

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Integrations">
      Go to **Settings > Integrations > Add Integration** and select **Notion**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Sign In to Notion">
      Sign in to your Notion account on the Notion authorization screen.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Select Pages and Databases">
      Notion prompts you to pick which pages and databases you want to share with Adimen AI. Select only the content you want Adimen AI to access, then click **Allow Access**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm">
      Return to Adimen AI to see your Notion integration listed as connected.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  **Available data:**
  - Content of selected pages (text, headings, lists)
  - Records and properties in selected databases

  <Note>
    Read access is granted by default, which allows Adimen AI to summarize and search your Notion content. Write access is optional — enable it if you want Adimen AI to create or update Notion pages through automations. You can adjust this at any time in **Settings > Integrations > Notion > Edit Permissions**.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="GitHub">
  Connect your GitHub account so Adimen AI can list your repositories, open issues, and pull requests. This is especially useful for generating daily standup summaries or tracking work-in-progress.

  **To connect GitHub:**

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Integrations">
      Go to **Settings > Integrations > Add Integration** and select **GitHub**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Authorize with GitHub">
      Sign in to GitHub and review the requested permissions. Adimen AI requests read-only access to your repositories, issues, and pull requests.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Select Repository Access">
      Choose whether to grant access to all repositories or only specific ones, then click **Authorize AdimenAI**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm">
      Return to Adimen AI — your GitHub account is now connected.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  **Available data:**
  - Repository names, descriptions, and visibility
  - Open and closed issues (title, status, assignee)
  - Pull requests (title, status, reviewers, branch names)

  <Note>
    Adimen AI does not read source code content. Only repository metadata, issue details, and pull request details are accessed.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Zapier">
  The Zapier integration lets you trigger Adimen AI automations from any of the 5,000+ apps supported by Zapier, using an outbound webhook URL generated inside Adimen AI.

  **To set up the Zapier integration:**

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Generate Your Webhook URL">
      Go to **Settings > Integrations > Zapier**. Tap **Generate Webhook URL** to create a unique endpoint for your account.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Copy the Webhook URL">
      Copy the generated URL — you will paste it into Zapier in the next step.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Create a Zap in Zapier">
      In your Zapier account, create a new Zap. Choose your trigger app and event, then add a **Webhooks by Zapier** action. Select **POST** and paste your Adimen AI webhook URL into the URL field.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Define the Payload">
      In the Zapier action, map the data fields you want to send to Adimen AI. Refer to the [Adimen AI Zapier payload reference](#) for supported fields.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Test and Activate">
      Test your Zap to confirm Adimen AI receives the webhook, then turn on the Zap.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Note>
    Each Adimen AI account has a single Zapier webhook URL. If you need to distinguish between multiple Zaps, include a custom `source` field in the payload to identify the triggering automation.
  </Note>
</Accordion>
