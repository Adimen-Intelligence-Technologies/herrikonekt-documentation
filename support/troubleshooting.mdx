If something isn't working as expected in Adimen AI, this guide walks you through the most common issues and how to resolve them quickly. Work through the steps for your issue category below — most problems are solved without needing to contact support.

<Tip>
  Before diving into specific issues, check [status.adimen.ai](https://status.adimen.ai) to see if there is an active service disruption. If Adimen AI's servers are experiencing an incident, waiting for the issue to resolve is often the fastest fix.
</Tip>

## Login & Account Issues

<Accordion title="Can't log in">
  If you're unable to sign in to your Adimen AI account, work through these steps in order:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check Your Credentials">
      Confirm you're entering the correct email address and password. Passwords are case-sensitive.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Use the Correct Login Method">
      Make sure you're using the same method you originally signed up with — **Email/Password**, **Continue with Google**, or **Continue with Apple**. Trying to sign in with a different method creates a separate account.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Reset Your Password">
      If you've forgotten your password, tap **Forgot Password?** on the login screen. You'll receive a reset link at your registered email address. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check Your Internet Connection">
      Adimen AI requires an active internet connection to authenticate. Ensure you have a working Wi-Fi or mobile data connection.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  If none of the above resolves the issue, [contact support](/support/contact) with your account email address.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Account locked">
  After **10 consecutive failed login attempts**, Adimen AI temporarily locks your account for **15 minutes** as a security measure. During this time, all login attempts will be rejected regardless of the password entered.

  **What to do:**
  - Wait 15 minutes, then try again.
  - Use **Forgot Password?** to reset your password before the lockout period ends — this will not extend the lockout.
  - If your account remains locked after 15 minutes or you believe the lockout was triggered by unauthorized access attempts, [contact support](/support/contact) immediately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Email verification not received">
  If you signed up with email but haven't received your verification email:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check Your Spam Folder">
      Verification emails sometimes land in junk or spam. Search your email for "Adimen" or check the Spam/Junk folder directly.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Add the Sender to Your Contacts">
      Add **support@adimen.ai** to your contacts or safe senders list to prevent future emails from being filtered.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Resend the Verification Email">
      On the login screen, tap **Resend Verification Email**. A new email will be sent to your registered address.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check the Email Address">
      Confirm you signed up with the email address you're checking. If you're unsure, try signing up again — if the email is already registered, you'll be prompted to sign in instead.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

## AI Features Not Working

<Accordion title="AI not responding">
  If the AI chat is loading indefinitely or returning no response:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check Your Internet Connection">
      AI features require an active internet connection. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data to rule out a network issue.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check the Status Bar">
      If a warning or error icon appears in the Adimen AI top bar, the app has detected a service issue. Tap the icon for details.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check Service Status">
      Visit [status.adimen.ai](https://status.adimen.ai) to see if there is an ongoing incident affecting AI services.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Restart the App">
      Force-close Adimen AI and reopen it to reset the connection to the AI service.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Incorrect or irrelevant AI responses">
  If the AI is giving responses that don't match what you asked:

  - **Rephrase your request.** More specific, direct prompts tend to produce better results. Instead of "tell me about my day," try "summarize my calendar events for today."
  - **Start a new session.** Long conversations can cause the AI to lose focus on your current intent. Tap the **New Chat** button to begin a fresh session with a clean context.
  - **Flag the response.** Tap the **thumbs down** icon below the response to report it as inaccurate or unhelpful. This directly informs model improvement.
  - **Check your integration data.** If the AI is giving outdated information from a connected service, verify the integration is still active in **Settings > Integrations**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Voice input not working">
  If the microphone button is unresponsive or voice input fails to transcribe:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check Microphone Permission">
      On iOS, go to **Settings > Adimen AI > Microphone** and make sure the toggle is on. On Android, go to **Settings > Apps > Adimen AI > Permissions > Microphone** and set it to **Allow**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Toggle the Permission Off and On">
      Sometimes refreshing the permission resolves transcription issues. Turn the microphone permission off, wait a few seconds, then turn it back on and reopen Adimen AI.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check for Conflicts">
      Make sure no other app is currently using the microphone. Close other apps that may be accessing the mic in the background.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Restart the App">
      Force-close and reopen Adimen AI, then try voice input again.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

## Sync Issues

<Accordion title="Data not syncing across devices">
  If changes made on one device aren't appearing on another:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Verify Cloud Sync Is Enabled">
      On both devices, go to **Settings > Account** and confirm the **Cloud Sync** toggle is on.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check Internet Connectivity">
      Sync requires an active internet connection on both devices. Confirm that both are online.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Look for the Sync Pending Icon">
      A sync icon in the top navigation bar indicates unsynced changes. Wait for it to clear, or force a sync by pulling down on the main screen to refresh.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Restart the App">
      Force-close Adimen AI on the affected device and reopen it. The app will attempt to sync immediately on launch.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Missing data after switching to a new device">
  If your history, settings, or saved items aren't appearing after signing in on a new device:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Confirm You're Signed Into the Same Account">
      Go to **Settings > Account** and verify the email address matches your original account. Signing in with a different method (e.g., Google instead of email) may create a separate account.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Wait for the Initial Sync to Complete">
      On a new device, the first full sync can take up to **5 minutes** depending on the volume of data in your account. Check the sync icon in the top bar and wait for it to finish.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check Your Device Limit">
      Free plan accounts support only 1 active device. If you exceeded your device limit, the oldest session may have been signed out automatically. Go to **Settings > Security > Active Sessions** to review your active devices.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

## Notifications Not Working

<Accordion title="Not receiving notifications">
  If Adimen AI isn't sending the notifications you expect, check each of the following:

  1. **System notification permissions** — Go to your device's system Settings and confirm Adimen AI has permission to send notifications.
     - *iOS:* **Settings > Adimen AI > Notifications > Allow Notifications**
     - *Android:* **Settings > Apps > Adimen AI > Notifications > Allow**
  2. **In-app notification settings** — In Adimen AI, go to **Settings > Notifications** and verify that the notification types you want are enabled.
  3. **Quiet Hours** — Check **Settings > Notifications > Quiet Hours** to confirm Quiet Hours isn't active during the times you expect to receive notifications.
  4. **System Do Not Disturb** — Make sure your device's Do Not Disturb or Focus mode isn't suppressing Adimen AI notifications.
  5. **Background app refresh** — On iOS, go to **Settings > General > Background App Refresh** and ensure it is enabled for Adimen AI.
</Accordion>

## Integration Issues

<Accordion title="Integration shows as disconnected">
  If a previously connected integration is showing a disconnected status:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Reconnect from Settings">
      Go to **Settings > Integrations**, tap the disconnected integration, and tap **Reconnect**. You will be prompted to re-authorize with the third-party service.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check the Source Service">
      The integration may have been revoked from the third-party service's side — for example, a password change or security event on your Google account can invalidate all third-party tokens. Sign in to the service and re-grant access.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Revoke and Reconnect">
      If reconnecting doesn't resolve the issue, fully disconnect the integration in **Settings > Integrations**, revoke access from the third-party service's permissions page, then set up the integration again from scratch.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Note>
    Some services (such as Slack workspace integrations) may require an admin to re-approve access after a disconnection. Check with your workspace admin if reconnection fails.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Info>
  If you've worked through the relevant steps above and the issue persists, our support team is ready to help. Visit the [Contact Support](/support/contact) page to reach us via in-app chat, email, or the community forum.
</Info>
