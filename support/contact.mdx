Whether you have a quick question or a complex issue, the Adimen AI support team is here to help. We offer several support channels — each suited to different types of requests. Choose the channel that matches your situation and subscription plan for the fastest resolution.

<Tip>
  In-app chat is the fastest way to get help. It connects you directly with the support team, gives agents instant access to your account context, and typically gets you a response faster than any other channel — especially on Pro and Business plans.
</Tip>

## Support Hours

Support availability depends on your subscription plan:

- **Pro and Business plans** — Support is available **24 hours a day, 7 days a week**, including holidays.
- **Free plan** — Support is available during **business hours: Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm UTC**.

Outside of business hours, Free plan users can still post in the [Community Forum](https://community.adimen.ai) for peer assistance.

## Response Times

| Plan | Channels | Response Time |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Email | 3 business days |
| Pro | In-app chat + Email | Within 24 hours |
| Business | Priority in-app chat + Email + Phone | 4-hour SLA |

<Note>
  Business plan customers receive a dedicated support queue and guaranteed 4-hour response SLA during all hours. If your Business plan request hasn't received a response within 4 hours, reply to the ticket thread to escalate.
</Note>

## What to Include in Your Request

Providing the right information upfront helps our team diagnose and resolve your issue much faster. Before reaching out, gather the following:

- **Account email** — the email address associated with your Adimen AI account
- **App version** — find this in **Settings > About > App Version**
- **Device model and OS version** — for example, "iPhone 15 Pro, iOS 17.4" or "Samsung Galaxy S24, Android 14"
- **Description of the issue** — what you were trying to do, what happened instead, and when it started
- **Steps to reproduce** — the exact steps that trigger the issue, so we can replicate it on our end
- **Error messages or screenshots** — if you see an error message or unexpected behavior, attach a screenshot or copy the exact error text

The more detail you include, the less back-and-forth is needed to reach a solution.
