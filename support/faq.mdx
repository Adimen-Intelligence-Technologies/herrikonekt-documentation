## Account & Subscription

<Accordion title="How do I change my subscription plan?">
  You can change your plan directly in the app or through your app store:

  - **In-app:** Go to **Settings > Subscription**, select the plan you want, and follow the prompts.
  - **App Store (iOS):** Open the App Store, tap your profile icon, select **Subscriptions**, find Adimen AI, and tap **Change Plan**.
  - **Google Play (Android):** Open Google Play, tap your profile icon, select **Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions**, find Adimen AI, and tap **Change plan**.

  Plan changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. You will not be charged or refunded for the current cycle.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use Adimen AI on multiple devices?">
  Yes — you can stay signed in on multiple devices at the same time, depending on your plan:

  | Plan | Active Devices |
  |---|---|
  | Free | 1 device |
  | Pro | Up to 5 devices |
  | Business | Unlimited |

  To view and manage your active devices, go to **Settings > Security > Active Sessions**. You can sign out of any individual device remotely from that screen.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
  Subscriptions are managed through the platform where you originally subscribed:

  - **iOS:** App Store > Profile > Subscriptions > Adimen AI > **Cancel Subscription**
  - **Android:** Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Adimen AI > **Cancel subscription**

  Adimen AI does not store your payment information directly — all billing is handled by Apple or Google. After cancellation, you retain access to your paid plan until the end of the current billing period.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a free trial for Pro?">
  Yes. When you first upgrade from the Free plan, you receive a **14-day free trial** of Adimen AI Pro. No credit card is required to start the trial. If you do not upgrade to a paid plan before the trial ends, your account automatically reverts to the Free plan and your data is preserved.
</Accordion>

## AI Features

<Accordion title="What languages does Adimen AI support?">
  Adimen AI currently supports the following languages for AI chat and recommendations:

  - English
  - Spanish
  - French
  - German
  - Portuguese
  - Italian
  - Japanese
  - Chinese (Simplified)

  The app interface is available in English. Additional language support is in active development — follow our release notes for updates.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Adimen AI work offline?">
  Some features work without an internet connection and some do not:

  - **Works offline:** App navigation, saved items, conversation history (read-only), and settings
  - **Requires internet:** AI chat, real-time recommendations, integration data, and automation execution

  When you go offline, the app displays your last-synced state. Any changes made offline are queued and synced automatically when connectivity is restored.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How accurate is the AI?">
  Adimen AI uses state-of-the-art large language models to generate responses. While the AI is highly capable across a broad range of tasks, it can make mistakes — particularly on rapidly changing information, highly specialized topics, or nuanced factual questions.

  For important or critical decisions, always verify AI-generated responses against authoritative sources. Use the thumbs down button on any response to flag inaccurate or unhelpful answers, which helps improve the model over time.
</Accordion>

## Privacy & Data

<Accordion title="Does Adimen AI sell my data?">
  No. Adimen AI does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Your data is used solely to operate and improve the Adimen AI service. For full details, see the [Adimen AI Privacy Policy](https://adimen.ai/privacy).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I delete my conversation history?">
  To clear your conversation history:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Privacy Settings">
      Go to **Settings > Privacy > Clear History**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Choose a Range">
      Select **All time** to delete everything, or pick a specific date range to delete only conversations from that period.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm Deletion">
      Tap **Clear History** and confirm when prompted. This action cannot be undone.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  Deleted conversation history is removed from all synced devices.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
  To permanently delete your Adimen AI account:

  <Steps>
    <Step title="Open Account Settings">
      Go to **Settings > Account > Delete Account**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Read the Warning">
      Review the list of data that will be permanently removed — this includes your profile, conversation history, saved items, automations, and integration connections.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm Deletion">
      Enter your password or complete the verification step, then tap **Delete My Account**. Deletion is immediate and cannot be reversed.
    </Step>
  </Steps>

  <Warning>
    Account deletion is permanent. All your data will be removed and cannot be recovered. If you are on a paid plan, cancel your subscription first through the App Store or Google Play to avoid future charges.
  </Warning>
</Accordion>

## Technical

<Accordion title="Why is the app slow?">
  Slow performance is usually caused by one of the following:

  1. **Poor internet connection** — AI features rely on a stable connection. Switch to a stronger Wi-Fi network or check your mobile data signal.
  2. **App needs a restart** — Force-close Adimen AI and reopen it. On iOS, swipe up from the Home bar and swipe away the app. On Android, use the recent apps button and swipe it off.
  3. **Outdated app version** — Make sure you have the latest version of Adimen AI installed via the App Store or Google Play.
  4. **Service disruption** — Check [status.adimen.ai](https://status.adimen.ai) to see if there is an ongoing incident.

  If slowness continues after trying these steps, visit [Troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting) or [contact support](/support/contact).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why aren't my notifications working?">
  Notification problems typically come from one of two places — device-level permissions or in-app settings:

  1. **Device permissions** — Go to your phone's **System Settings > Apps > Adimen AI > Notifications** and make sure notifications are allowed. On iOS, check **Settings > Adimen AI > Notifications**.
  2. **In-app settings** — Open Adimen AI and go to **Settings > Notifications**. Verify the notification types you want are enabled.
  3. **Quiet Hours** — Check whether Quiet Hours is active in **Settings > Notifications > Quiet Hours**, which silences all app notifications during a defined time window.
  4. **Do Not Disturb** — Make sure your phone's system-level Do Not Disturb mode is not blocking Adimen AI.
</Accordion>
